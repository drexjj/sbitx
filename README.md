# A MESSAGE TO THE SBITX COMMUNITY

I've invested countless hours developing, improving, testing, and supporting software for the sBitx and zBitx platforms, much of which I've made freely available to the amateur radio community. I've done this because I believe in contributing to the community and helping the sBitx ecosystem grow.

Unfortunately, some folks seem to have mistaken that generosity for permission to take other people's work, rebrand it, and present it as their own.

So let's make something clear.

**If you're using, modifying, or incorporating software derived from our work, you are expected to comply with the applicable licenses and attribution requirements.**

Beyond the licensing obligations, there's also a basic expectation of professional courtesy: give credit where credit is due.

I've contributed thousands of hours to the sBitx ecosystem, including software improvements, applications, toolbox utilities, hardware calibration, digital mode integrations, remote control functionality, and countless usability enhancements. All of this has been contributed freely for the benefit of the community.

I'm not asking anyone to stop using my work. That's precisely why I've shared it. I encourage others to build upon it, improve it, and take it further.

**But open source does not mean the original developers should become invisible.**

There is a fundamental difference between building upon someone else's contributions and removing their identity while presenting that work as your own.

Unfortunately, the continued lack of attribution and recognition has reached a point where I've had to reconsider the direction of this project.

**As a result, further development of my sBitx software project has shifted to a new, closed-source development effort.** This new software will still be offered free of charge to the sBitx community. My decision to move away from open-source development is not about money; it's about protecting the time, effort, and intellectual contributions that go into this work.

My existing zBitx repositories and software will remain online and accessible for now. I intend to continue developing zBitx separately, and its future direction will remain independent of this decision.
